About

Example: a locomotive horn sounded at a grade crossing. US rules (49 CFR 229.129) require 96–110 dB(A) at 100 ft in front of the locomotive; the map location is illustrative.

Free-field estimate: no terrain, buildings, ground reflection, wind or temperature effects. Real levels can differ by several dB. The figures are not measurements.

Same formula as the Train Horn decibel distance calculator. Source code and API: GitHub · smoke test.
